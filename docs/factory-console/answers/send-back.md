# Send it back

`send-back` is one of four answers on every delivery ask: send-back, replan, take-as-is and hold.

What starts next: the same builder continues on the change, in the checkout the asking run left, with what you write in your answer.

What it costs: one more delivery run.

Pick it when the change is close and your answer can say what to fix. Pick `replan` to send the item back to planning, which may discard the work so far. Pick `take-as-is` to build nothing more, or `hold` to start nothing until an operator says more.
